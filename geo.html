(01) What GEO is

SEO gets you found. GEO gets you quoted.

Search used to end in a list of links. Increasingly it ends in a single answer, and that answer names a few brands.

Traditional SEO optimises for ten blue links. GEO optimises for the single answer a user reads without clicking. When someone asks an AI assistant which platform, vendor or approach to trust, you want your brand in that response, with a citation back to you.

Research on generative answer engines shows that pages adding clear statistics, expert quotes and authoritative citations can see materially higher inclusion in AI responses, even when they do not rank first in classic search. GEO is how you capture that gap.

In practice, that means making your site easy for AI crawlers to read, shaping your pages into passages a model can lift and quote, and building the third-party signals that tell a model your brand is a credible source.

(07) Timing & your part

When citations start to move

Each engine re-reads the web at its own pace, so results arrive in waves rather than all at once.

Structural fixes show up first, because some engines cite their sources openly on every answer. Changes in search-grounded answers follow as crawlers re-index your updated pages. Off-site authority takes longest, but its impact is the most durable, because it changes how the whole web describes you.

From your side, the time commitment is light. We need:

  • A kickoff session on your buyers, competitors and the questions you want to own
  • Access to your website or CMS, analytics and robots.txt
  • Your expertise, data and opinions, so content carries real authority
  • Approval of key content before it goes live, and a short recurring review

(09) FAQ

Common GEO questions

Still unsure? Book a call and we'll answer them for your business specifically.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO targets rankings in traditional search results. GEO targets inclusion and citation inside AI-generated answers. You still need fast pages, clean URLs and indexability, but GEO adds answer blocks, schema markup, entity authority and prompt-level monitoring on top.

How long until we see citation improvements?

Perplexity often reflects structural fixes within days because it cites transparently on every answer. ChatGPT search-grounded responses typically shift over two to six weeks as crawlers re-index updated pages. Off-site authority work can take longer but has durable impact across all engines.

Do we need llms.txt?

It is optional. llms.txt is a lightweight convention that points AI tools to your most important pages. It takes minutes to publish and may help as adoption grows, but it is not a replacement for robots.txt crawler rules, structured data, or high-quality crawlable content, which are the levers that matter most today.

How does GEO fit with your other services?

GEO is the AI visibility layer inside our inbound system. It works alongside SEO, content and performance marketing so discovery compounds. Outbound then converts the demand GEO and inbound create. Most clients run GEO as part of a connected growth programme, not in isolation.

Can you guarantee we will be cited?

No one can honestly guarantee what an AI model will say, because the engines decide what to cite and they change often. What we can do is remove the reasons you are being left out, build the signals models rely on, and measure your citations every month so you can see exactly what is moving.

What do you need from us?

A kickoff session on your buyers and competitors, access to your website, analytics and robots.txt, and your expertise so content carries real authority. You approve key content before it goes live; we handle the rest and keep you aligned on a recurring cadence.

Can we check where we stand before talking to you?

Yes. Our free AI readiness checker shows whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI can find, read and cite your website, plus a classic SEO health check. It covers the technical side; a GEO audit adds the prompt panel and competitor citation picture.
