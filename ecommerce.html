Three connected layers that find buyers, measure what they're worth and keep them coming back, each one feeding the next.

OutcomeGrowth that feeds itself

Most ecommerce brands grow by renting attention: more ad spend in, more sales out, until costs rise or tracking breaks. We take a different approach. We build growth infrastructure into your business, a set of systems you own that keep working when ad platforms change their rules.

It starts with data. Once you can see what each channel really earns after costs, every other decision gets easier: where to put budget, which products to push in search, which customers to bring back and when.

On top of that foundation we add the acquisition and automation layers: AI SEO and high-intent paid traffic to bring in ready-to-buy shoppers, automated campaigns that move budget towards what is actually profitable, and retention systems that turn first orders into repeat customers.

Together they form a self-reinforcing way of acquiring customers rather than a series of short-lived campaign spikes.

(02) Why it matters

What's holding growth back

Most brands try to scale on ad channels alone. If tracking is shaky underneath, more spend just multiplies the problems you already have.

  1. 01

    Rising acquisition costs

    More competition on the ad platforms and ongoing privacy changes keep pushing up the cost of each new customer, eating straight into gross margin.

  2. 02

    ROAS you can't rely on

    As single-pixel tracking data gets worse, reported return on ad spend swings wildly, which makes it hard to plan or invest with confidence.

  3. 03

    Attribution nobody agrees on

    Each platform reports different numbers for the same sales. Budget ends up following whichever dashboard shouts loudest, not what really works.

  4. 04

    Weak retention

    Without systems that respond to customer behaviour after the first purchase, brands end up paying again and again to win back customers they already had.

If your foundations are unstable, scaling ad spend doesn't fix them. It makes them more expensive. Fix the data first, then grow on top of it

(04) Measurement

One version of the truth

Dashboarding and revenue analytics show which channels are actually profitable, not which ones are best at claiming credit.

Most growing brands read their numbers in four or five places: Meta Ads Manager, Google Analytics, Shopify reports, the email platform. Each counts sales differently. The result is double-counted purchases, distorted attribution and a blended ROAS nobody fully trusts.

We build server-side tracking that links every unit of marketing spend to real net revenue. Because it doesn't rely on the browser pixel, it isn't knocked out by iOS privacy changes or cookie restrictions.

Everything lands in one clean reporting layer. Your team sees real acquisition cost, how much each channel really contributes and actual margin, so budget decisions are faster, more confident and protected from the over-reporting built into ad platform dashboards.

(05) Retention & lifecycle

Stop buying back your customers

After the first order, the system follows each customer's journey and sends the right offer at the right moment, automatically.

Example post-purchase journeyFig. 03

  1. Trigger · First orderOrder placed

    The customer's timeline starts: what they bought, when, and how they found you.

  2. Trigger · DeliveryFulfilment touchpoints

    Messages timed to the order and delivery, not to a marketing calendar.

  3. Trigger · BehaviourComplementary product

    A recommendation based on what they bought and how they've interacted.

  4. Trigger · Product lifecycleReplenishment reminder

    Sent when the product is likely to be running out.

  5. Trigger · InactivityWin-back sequence

    A relevant reason to return for customers who have gone quiet.

  6. GoalRepeat purchase

    Higher lifetime value, without blanket discounting.

Most brands lean on calendar-based broadcast emails. Over time that leads to list fatigue, more unsubscribes and a weaker sender reputation, all of which reduce the value of the customer list you've already paid to build.

Our retention systems treat each buyer as an individual, not as a fresh acquisition target. They track buyer timelines, how long each product lasts, how often people buy and how they've interacted with you, then send the relevant offer at the right moment, without anyone managing campaigns by hand.

Repeat purchases come from relevance rather than discounts. That raises lifetime value, reduces how much you depend on buying new customers and strengthens net margin across the business.

(07) How we set it up

Four phases to stable growth

We fix the measurement first, then launch acquisition, then scale what works. Each phase builds on the one before.

  1. 01

    Audit

    We go through your past performance, tracking gaps, attribution mismatches and how different customer groups behave over time, to find the problems that are actually holding you back.

  2. 02

    Infrastructure setup

    We build your custom dashboards, set up server-side attribution tracking and get your analytics data clean and consistent.

  3. 03

    AI campaign systems

    We launch automated search clustering (grouping related searches into pages that rank) alongside AI-automated campaigns, to win high-intent customers from both organic and paid.

  4. 04

    Scaling & optimisation

    We fine-tune the behaviour-based retention loops and put ongoing conversion optimisation in place to keep margins healthy as you grow.

  • Access to your store platform, ad accounts and analytics
  • Access to your email or messaging platform for lifecycle flows
  • A named person to approve campaigns and creative direction
  • A short regular review of the dashboard together

Everything we build is set up in your own accounts, so you keep full ownership of the data and the systems.

(09) FAQ

Ecommerce questions we get most

Still unsure? Book a call and we'll answer them for your brand specifically.

What is AI SEO for ecommerce brands?

It goes well beyond tracking keywords by hand and writing product meta descriptions. We use AI models to map and organise your site structure across thousands of product categories, read how buyer search behaviour is changing, and create relevant landing pages at scale that meet what modern search engines reward.

The focus is on transactional searches, the ones people make when they're ready to buy rather than browse. That brings steady organic traffic to your store, lowers acquisition costs over time and reduces your dependence on paid channels. Unlike paid ads, which stop the moment budgets are cut, organic search builds up and keeps generating revenue without extra spend.

How does dashboarding improve ecommerce growth?

It gives leadership one place to see what's really happening. Most scaling brands read metrics across Meta Ads Manager, Google Analytics, Shopify reports and email platforms, which leads to distorted attribution, purchases counted twice and blended ROAS you can't rely on. That makes it nearly impossible to know which channels are actually profitable.

We build server-side data pipelines that link every unit of marketing spend to real net revenue, working around the browser pixel limits caused by iOS privacy changes and cookie restrictions. With everything in one clean reporting layer, your team sees real acquisition cost, how much each channel contributes and true margin, so budget decisions are faster, more confident and protected from the over-reporting built into ad platform dashboards.

What are AI automated campaigns and how do they work?

They are paid campaigns that keep optimising budget in real time without someone adjusting them by hand. Standard campaigns run on fixed rules and weekly changes, which means slow creative testing, wasted spend at the wrong times and missed chances to scale when a creative starts winning.

Our system constantly balances creative variations, audience targeting and bids across your marketing mix, based on real-time margin data rather than vanity metrics. It matches patterns to buyer behaviour, moves budget towards the best combinations and pauses weak ones before they waste spend. The result is less creative fatigue, lower cost per acquisition over time and efficient acquisition as you increase spend.

How do you drive high-intent traffic that actually converts?

By moving away from broad, untargeted audiences towards targeting built around active purchase signals. Many brands waste a lot of spend on cheap, unqualified traffic that produces impressions but not sales, making reach look good while ROAS suffers.

We filter for shoppers showing clear buying signals: people searching commercial-intent keywords, engaging with competitor product pages, or behaving in ways consistent with buying soon. We combine search optimisation with exact-match and shopping ads so your products appear at the moment of need. Spend is concentrated on audiences who are ready to buy, which lifts conversion rates and ROAS across the whole growth engine.

How does Growth Loop improve retention and lifetime value?

We install automated systems that follow each customer's journey after the first purchase, instead of treating every buyer as a new acquisition target. Relying only on calendar-based broadcast emails causes list fatigue, rising unsubscribes and a weaker sender reputation, all of which reduce the value of your customer list.

Our retention systems track buyer timelines, product lifecycles, purchase frequency and past interactions to send relevant offers at the right moment, automatically: replenishment reminders, complementary product recommendations and win-back sequences, with no constant manual campaign work. Encouraging repeat purchases through relevance rather than blanket discounts raises lifetime value, reduces how exposed you are to acquisition costs and strengthens net margin.

Do we have to stop running paid ads?

No. Paid traffic stays part of the acquisition layer, but it is run on high-intent targeting and real margin data. What changes is the dependency: as organic search equity and retention build up, more of your revenue comes from assets you own rather than from spend you have to keep renewing.

Who owns the data and dashboards?
  • You do: 100% data ownership.
  • Tracking, dashboards and customer data are set up in your own accounts.
  • Reporting is independent of any ad platform, so there is zero platform bias in how channels are judged.
Where do we start?

With the audit. We review your historical performance, tracking gaps, attribution mismatches and how customer groups behave over time, then show you which problems are really limiting growth. From there we build the infrastructure in the order that matters for your brand, starting with data.
