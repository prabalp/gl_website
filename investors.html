Frequently Asked Questions

Traditional deal origination relies on inbound pitch decks, broker-intermediated processes, and personal network referrals, all of which are reactive, slow, and shared across the entire market simultaneously. By the time a company reaches your inbox through conventional channels, multiple competing funds are already evaluating the same opportunity, compressing your negotiating position and driving up entry multiples.

AI-powered deal sourcing replaces this reactive posture with continuous, programmatic data capture. We install active monitoring systems that track early-stage company signals across web infrastructure, hiring databases, product usage footprints, and regulatory filings. This surfaces high-potential targets weeks or months before they seek formal institutional backing, giving your fund a direct, low-competition path to first conversation. The result is a proprietary deal flow engine that runs independently of market noise and belongs entirely to your firm.

Our startup intelligence framework monitors secondary indicators across fragmented data sources that most investment teams do not have the infrastructure to track systematically. This includes corporate filings, job posting velocity and seniority patterns, technology stack migrations, domain registration activity, app store momentum, and organic web traffic growth, all of which reveal real operational traction before any financial metrics are self-reported or audited.

These raw signals are cross-referenced and scored against your specific investment thesis parameters in real time. The output is a continuously refreshed, ranked list of companies exhibiting the growth characteristics your mandate is looking for, presented through a unified dashboard rather than scattered across spreadsheets. Your investment team receives actionable intelligence, not raw data, allowing analysts to spend time on high-conviction diligence rather than manual research and scanning.

Speed is a competitive advantage in early-stage deal sourcing. Even when a fund identifies a strong target ahead of the market, delays in initial outreach, caused by manual research, CRM data entry, or partner availability, can allow competing funds to establish the first relationship. First mover in founder conversations correlates directly with better deal terms and allocation priority.

Our automated outreach infrastructure triggers personalised, contextually relevant messages to qualified targets the moment growth signals cross your scoring threshold. These are not generic templated emails. They reference specific company milestones, product developments, or market events that make the outreach feel researched and credible. All interactions are logged directly into your deal pipeline, with responses routed to the appropriate partner or analyst for follow-up. This compresses the time from signal detection to first founder conversation from weeks to days.
