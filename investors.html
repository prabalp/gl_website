(01) What it is

Deal flow that comes to you first

A system that moves your sourcing from waiting for decks to finding the companies yourself, on your thesis, ahead of the market.

Most funds source the same way: inbound decks, broker lists and personal networks. All three are reactive, and all three are shared with every other fund in your market. By the time a company lands in your inbox, others are already looking at it.

We replace that with continuous, programmatic sourcing. The system monitors early company signals across the web, hiring data, product usage and regulatory filings, and scores every company against your investment thesis.

The strongest matches appear, ranked, in one dashboard. Qualified founders receive a personalised first message from you, often weeks or months before they look for formal institutional backing, giving you a direct, low-competition route to the first conversation.

(05) Signals & scoring

Traction before the deck

Real operating momentum shows up in public signals long before any financials are self-reported or audited.

Our startup intelligence monitors signals spread across sources most investment teams can't track systematically:

  • Corporate and regulatory filings
  • Job posting velocity and the seniority of new hires
  • Technology stack changes
  • Domain registrations and executive moves
  • App store momentum and organic web traffic growth

These signals are cross-checked and scored against your thesis in real time. The output is a continuously updated, ranked list of companies showing the growth characteristics your mandate looks for, in one dashboard rather than scattered spreadsheets.

Your team gets actionable intelligence, not raw data, so analysts spend their time on high-conviction diligence instead of manual research.

(09) FAQ

Investor questions we get most

Still unsure? Book a call and we'll answer them for your fund specifically.

What is AI-powered deal sourcing, and how is it different?

Traditional origination relies on inbound decks, broker-led processes and personal referrals. All are reactive, slow and shared with the whole market at once. By the time a company reaches your inbox, several competing funds are already evaluating it, which weakens your negotiating position and pushes up entry multiples.

AI-powered sourcing replaces this with continuous, programmatic data capture. We set up monitoring that tracks early company signals across the web, hiring databases, product usage and regulatory filings. It surfaces high-potential targets weeks or months before they seek formal institutional backing, giving you a direct, low-competition path to the first conversation. The result is a proprietary deal flow engine that runs independently of market noise and belongs entirely to your firm.

How does startup intelligence work in practice?

It monitors secondary indicators across scattered sources that most investment teams can't track systematically: corporate filings, job posting velocity and seniority, technology stack changes, domain registrations, app store momentum and organic web traffic growth. Together these reveal real traction before any financials are self-reported or audited.

The signals are cross-checked and scored against your thesis in real time. You get a continuously refreshed, ranked list of companies that fit your mandate, in one dashboard rather than scattered spreadsheets, so analysts spend their time on high-conviction diligence instead of manual research.

Can you help filter and prioritise inbound deal flow too?

Yes, it's a significant part of what we build. Most funds get far more applications than they can properly evaluate, which leads either to blanket rejections that damage founder relationships or to deep dives that burn analyst time on misaligned deals.

  • Every submission is scored against your criteria: geography, sector, revenue stage, business model, team background and technology fit.
  • High-signal submissions are flagged and queued for partner review.
  • Mid-tier ones are logged and monitored for future relevance.
  • Clearly misaligned ones are filtered out without manual work.

Your team focuses where conviction is highest, and every founder still gets a professional, structured process.

How does automated outreach speed up sourcing?

Speed is a competitive advantage. Even when you spot a strong company early, delays from manual research, CRM data entry or partner availability can let another fund build the first relationship, and being first to the founder conversation goes hand in hand with better terms and allocation priority.

Our outreach sends personalised, relevant messages the moment a company crosses your scoring threshold. They aren't generic templates: they reference specific milestones, product developments or market events, so they read as researched and credible. Every interaction is logged in your deal pipeline and replies go to the right partner or analyst. This cuts the time from signal to first founder conversation from weeks to days.

What types of investment firms is this for?

The infrastructure works for any mandate and is configured to each fund type:

  • Early-stage VC: startup intelligence and founder signal tracking to find breakthrough companies at pre-seed and seed, before pricing gets competitive.
  • Growth equity and PE: outbound origination to find off-market acquisition and minority targets, avoiding auctions entirely.
  • Family offices: private market monitoring for long-term direct positions aligned to wealth preservation and sector concentration rules, with full confidentiality.
  • Angel networks and syndicates: filtering and scoring to manage high application volume and surface the strongest co-investments for members.
  • Strategic corporate venture and M&A teams: market scanning to track complementary technology assets and emerging category leaders relevant to your roadmap.
Is our sourcing kept confidential?

Yes. The system is built for your firm alone and belongs entirely to you. Your thesis, targets and pipeline are not disclosed to any network, which is why family offices use it for direct investments that require full confidentiality.

Will founders see this as spam?

No. Outreach only goes to verified companies that match your thesis, and each message references something specific to that company, such as a milestone, product launch or market event. It reads as researched interest from your fund, not a template, and the reply comes straight to a partner or analyst.
