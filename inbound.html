(01) What it is

An engine, not a content calendar

Inbound works when discovery, capture and handoff are built as one connected system.

Most inbound efforts stall for the same reasons: content that nobody finds, traffic that leaves without a trace, and leads that sit in an inbox until they go cold. An inbound system connects every step so none of that happens.

First, we make you discoverable wherever buyers look today: in Google results, inside the answers of AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, and in paid placements targeted with data from your own organic traffic.

Then we make sure that attention turns into pipeline. Lead magnets give visitors a reason to share their details, a website chatbot answers questions and qualifies them on the spot, and every lead is handed straight to your sales team or CRM.

(04) How the pillars connect

One buyer, five touchpoints

Buyers rarely convert on a single visit. Here is how the pillars hand a prospect from one to the next.

A modern buyer might first meet you inside an AI answer, check you out on Google a week later, see an ad, and only then download something or ask a question. If each channel is run in isolation, that path breaks at every step.

In an inbound system, each pillar feeds the next. GEO and SEO create the first impression. Performance marketing retargets the people who showed interest, using what your organic traffic tells us about them. Lead magnets and the chatbot capture intent the moment it appears.

By the time someone reaches your sales team, they already know who you are, they have read your thinking, and their questions and history are in your CRM.

(06) How the inbound engine runs

Audit, build, publish, refine

Four stages, then a monthly loop of review. You approve direction; we handle execution.

  1. 01

    Discovery audit

    A search and GEO visibility scan shows where you appear today, in Google and in AI answers. A competitor content gap map shows the topics and questions your rivals own and you don't.

  2. 02

    Build assets

    We create the RA reports, lead magnets and chatbot, and set up the GEO and SEO content structure so every page is easy to rank, cite and convert from.

  3. 03

    Publish & promote

    Content goes live and gets indexed. Meta ads then amplify the top performers, so paid budget only goes behind what is already proving itself.

  4. 04

    Rank & refine

    A monthly ranking and traffic review shows what is working. We double down on what compounds and cut what doesn't.

(09) FAQ

Inbound questions we get most

Still unsure? Book a call and we'll answer them for your business specifically.

How long before inbound produces real traffic?
  • GEO and lead magnets can show early signal within four to six weeks.
  • SEO and RA reports typically compound over two to four months.
  • Performance marketing fills the gap with immediate volume while organic ramps.
What does GEO actually change about our content?
  • Content is structured for AI citation, not just ranked links.
  • Claims are made verifiable with original data.
  • Brand mentions are built consistently across the web.

See the full five-phase GEO framework.

Do we need a large content team to run this?
  • No. We build and run the system end to end.
  • It is built specifically for one to five person teams.
  • You approve direction, we handle execution.
What is a Ranked Authority (RA) report?

It is a piece of content built to be the reference point in your category, not just another blog post. It is designed to be the page buyers land on when they research your category, alongside industry and competitor comparison pages, so they meet your thinking before they meet a salesperson.

Do we have to run paid ads?

No, but it helps. Organic assets take time to compound, and performance marketing fills the gap with immediate volume while they ramp. Because we target ads using data from your own organic traffic and only amplify content that is already performing, the budget goes behind what works.

Should we do inbound or outbound first?

Usually both. Outbound starts conversations this month with the accounts you choose; inbound compounds over months and lowers your cost per lead. Running them together gives you speed now and a pipeline that grows on its own later.

Who owns the content, leads and data?

You do. Content is published on your website, ads run in your account, and every captured lead and chatbot conversation is handed into your own CRM.

How do you measure success?

By qualified leads and the sales conversations they create, not by page views alone. The monthly review covers rankings, AI visibility, traffic and captured leads, so it is clear what is compounding and where we are shifting effort.
