(01) The loop

One cycle, four phases

Every engagement runs the same loop: understand the business, design the future state, build it with your team, then use the results to start the next cycle from a better position.

Most growth work is sold as a project with an end date. A strategy is delivered, something is launched, and the learning stops there. Within months the market moves and the plan is out of date.

The Growth Loop is built the other way round. Launch is not the finish line; it is the point where real data starts arriving. We use that data to refine the strategy, then run the loop again.

Each pass through the loop starts with better information than the last, which is why your business becomes more efficient and more profitable with every cycle.

(02) The four phases

What happens, what you get

Each phase has a clear job and a clear set of outputs, so you always know where we are in the loop and what comes next.

Phase 01

Discovery & Diagnostic

We begin by immersing ourselves in your business. We audit your data, interview your stakeholders, and map your current processes. We identify not just the symptoms, but the root causes of your stagnation.

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Tech stack audit
  • Competitor analysis
  • A map of current processes and the root causes holding growth back
Phase 02

Strategic Architecture

We design the future state. This isn't just a slide deck; it's a blueprint. We define the specific OKRs, resource requirements, and technological changes needed to achieve your growth targets.

  • 3-year strategic roadmap
  • Financial modelling
  • KPI definition
  • OKRs, resource requirements and the technology changes needed
Phase 03

Agile Execution

We work alongside your teams to implement the changes. Whether it's standing up a new data warehouse, training your sales team, or deploying an AI voice agent, we are in the trenches with you.

  • Sprint-based implementation
  • Change management
  • Regular progress reviews
  • Live systems your team is trained to run
Phase 04

Optimisation Loop

Launch is just the beginning. We use the data generated from the execution phase to refine the strategy. This feedback loop ensures that your business becomes more efficient and more profitable with every cycle.

  • Automated dashboards
  • Quarterly business reviews
  • New opportunity identification
  • A refined strategy that becomes the input to the next cycle
(03) Why a loop

Projects end. Systems compound.

A one-off project gives you a snapshot. A loop gives you a business that keeps learning from its own results.

  1. 01

    Root causes, not symptoms

    Discovery looks past the visible problem, such as too few meetings, to the cause underneath, such as a weak ICP, disconnected data or a manual process that doesn't scale.

  2. 02

    A blueprint, not a deck

    Strategy is translated into specific OKRs, KPIs, resources and technology changes, so execution starts with clear targets rather than broad ambitions.

  3. 03

    Built with your team

    We implement alongside the people who will run the system, so knowledge stays in your business once a sprint is done.

  4. 04

    Every cycle starts higher

    Real results from execution replace assumptions. The next round of decisions is based on what actually happened in your market.

Launch is just the beginning. The data a system generates is the most valuable input to the next version of it

(07) FAQ

Questions about the approach

Still unsure? Book a call and we'll walk through how the loop would run in your business.

Do you take on one-off projects?

We don't do "one-and-done" projects. Every engagement is designed as a loop: we build a system, measure what it produces and use that data to improve it. That is how a system keeps getting smarter after launch instead of slowly going out of date.

What exactly do we get from the discovery and strategy phases?
  • From Discovery & Diagnostic: stakeholder interviews, a tech stack audit, competitor analysis and a clear view of the root causes behind your stagnation.
  • From Strategic Architecture: a 3-year strategic roadmap, financial modelling and KPI definitions, plus the OKRs, resources and technology changes needed to hit your targets.
Is the strategy just a slide deck?

No. It's a blueprint. It defines specific OKRs, resource requirements and technological changes, and it is the plan we then implement with you in sprints. We stay involved through execution, so the strategy is tested against reality rather than handed over and left.

What does our team need to do?

Give us access to stakeholders and data during discovery, sign off the blueprint, name an owner for execution and make people available for training. After that, the main commitment is taking part in regular progress reviews and quarterly business reviews.

Do you work alongside our existing team?

Yes. We work alongside your teams to implement the changes, whether that's standing up a new data warehouse, training your sales team or deploying an AI voice agent. Change management is part of execution, so new systems are adopted rather than ignored.

Do we have to start with all six systems?

No. Discovery shows where the biggest constraint is, and the roadmap sequences the work. Many engagements start with one system, such as outbound or revenue analytics, and add others in later cycles as new opportunities are identified.

How do we know it's working?

KPIs are defined in the strategy phase, before anything is built. Automated dashboards track them continuously, regular progress reviews cover each sprint, and quarterly business reviews compare results against the plan and decide what the next cycle should focus on.
