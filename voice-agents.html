(01) What it is

A phone line that never goes unanswered

An AI voice agent holds a natural spoken conversation, does the routine work of the call and writes everything back to your systems.

Most businesses lose calls in the gaps: after hours, at lunch, when the team is already on another line, or when a new lead waits days for a callback. Each of those is a conversation someone wanted to have with you.

An AI voice agent answers or places those calls for you. It listens, understands what the caller needs, answers from information you have approved, asks your qualifying questions and takes the next step: booking a meeting, sending a confirmation or passing the call to a person.

It is not a phone menu and it is not a replacement for your team. It handles the repeatable parts of a call consistently, every time, and hands over to a human when the conversation needs judgement, care or a decision.

(03) An example call

Natural, brief and to the point

Here is the shape of a typical inbound enquiry. Your agent's script, questions and tone are designed with you.

A good voice agent sounds like a capable member of your front-desk team: friendly, clear and focused on helping the caller get what they need.

It follows your playbook. The questions it asks, what it is allowed to say, which slots it can offer and when it must hand over are all agreed before launch.

It says what it is. The agent can introduce itself as an AI assistant, and if the call is recorded it tells the caller where disclosure is required.

When the call ends, your team does not need to listen back. A short summary, the answers given and the booking land in your CRM straight away.

(04) How it works

Five parts working in real time

Under the hood, a voice agent is a chain of components that listen, think, speak and act, fast enough to feel like a normal conversation.

The voice agent stack, in plain wordsFig. 03

01 · Phone lineConnects the call

Your existing number forwards to the agent, or we set up a new one.

02 · ListeningSpeech to text

The caller's words are transcribed as they speak.

03 · ThinkingLanguage model + your playbook

Decides what to say next, using only your approved knowledge and rules.

04 · SpeakingText to speech

A natural voice replies, in the tone and language you choose.

05 · ActingTools & integrations

Checks calendars, books slots, updates the CRM, transfers calls.

  1. 01

    A playbook, not a free-for-all

    We write the agent's goals, questions, approved answers and hard limits with you. It does not improvise prices, promises or policy.

  2. 02

    Knowledge you control

    Opening hours, services, FAQs and booking rules come from a source you can update, so the agent stays current without a rebuild.

  3. 03

    Real actions, not just talk

    The agent uses defined tools: look up a slot, create a booking, update a record, send a text, transfer a call. Nothing outside that list.

The model is the voice. The playbook, the knowledge and the integrations are what make it useful to your business. That is the part we engineer with you
(05) Human handoff

Built to hand over, not hold on

The agent handles the routine. Your people handle anything that needs judgement, empathy or a decision, with the context already in hand.

  1. 01

    The caller asks for a person

    They are offered a transfer or a callback straight away. Nobody gets trapped in a loop.

  2. 02

    The call is complex or sensitive

    Complaints, distress, negotiations or anything outside the playbook goes to your team.

  3. 03

    The opportunity is high value

    You set the rules, for example: any qualified enterprise lead is transferred live to sales.

  4. 04

    The agent is not sure

    Rather than guess, it says it will get the right person and takes the details.

(08) How we launch it

From first call to live line

Five stages, then ongoing tuning. Timelines depend on the use case and integrations, and are scoped with you on the first call.

    Discovery

    We map the calls you get or want to make, what a good outcome looks like, and which systems the agent needs to reach.

  2. 02

    Design & scripts

  3. 03

(10) FAQ

Voice agent questions we get most

Still unsure? Book a call and we'll answer them for your business specifically.

Will it sound robotic?

Modern voice agents sound natural, respond quickly and cope with interruptions far better than older phone systems. We choose the voice, pace and wording with you and test with real calls before launch. Callers can still tell it is an assistant, which is why we recommend the agent says so openly.

Will it replace my team?
  • No. It takes the repetitive, high-volume parts of calls: answering, routine questions, qualifying and booking.
  • Complex, sensitive or high-value calls are handed to your people with a summary.
  • Your team spends more time on the conversations that need them.
What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

It does not guess. It tells the caller it will get the right person, then transfers the call or takes the details and books a callback. Each of these moments is reviewed afterwards, so we can add the missing information or adjust the rules.

Which CRMs and phone systems does it work with?

Most mainstream CRMs, calendars and phone setups, plus many tools that offer an API. You can usually keep your existing number and forward calls to the agent. We confirm the exact integrations in discovery, before you commit to a build.

Are calls recorded, and is that allowed?

Recording is optional. Where calls are recorded, the agent tells the caller where disclosure is required, and recordings and transcripts are stored in systems you control with the retention you choose. For outbound calling, we design around consent, opt-outs and do-not-call lists. You should confirm the specific rules that apply in your markets with your own advisers.

How long does it take to launch?

It depends on the use case and how many systems the agent needs to connect to. A single, well-defined job, such as after-hours answering and booking, is much quicker than a multi-step qualification flow across several tools. We scope a realistic timeline with you on the first call.

What do you need from us?

A discovery session on the calls you get and the outcomes you want, the information the agent should answer from (services, hours, FAQs, booking rules), access to your calendar, CRM and phone setup, and someone to review the script and test calls. After launch, a short regular review.

How do you measure success?

By outcomes, not minutes: calls answered that would have been missed, leads qualified, meetings booked, issues resolved, and how cleanly calls are handed to your team. We pair the numbers with regular reviews of real calls, because quality matters as much as volume.
