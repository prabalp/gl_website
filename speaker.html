(05) Anatomy of a message

An idea, not a brochure

Buyers ignore "available for bookings". They reply to a specific idea for their specific situation.

Every outreach message presents a tailored concept: what you would do for this company, tied to something real that is happening there. No mass platform spam, no copy-and-paste speaker sheets.

Because we know the trigger, such as fast hiring, a new leader or a culture programme, the message reads like it was written for one person. Because it was.

When a buyer replies, the conversation is passed to you to run the discovery call. You do what you do best; we keep the pipeline full.

(09) FAQ

Questions experts ask us

Still unsure? Book a call and we'll answer them for your practice specifically.

How is this different from a speaker bureau or talent agency?

Bureaus and talent agencies work as passive listings. They wait for event organisers to call, represent dozens or hundreds of speakers at once and take a significant commission on every booking, often 20 to 30 percent. Your visibility depends on how prominently they feature you, so you have little control over your pipeline or your positioning.

We work as your dedicated, proactive business development engine. We build your prospect database, write personalised outreach and contact corporate decision-makers on your behalf every week. The data, relationships and pipeline belong entirely to you. This is not speaker representation; it is speaker business development at scale.

Which corporate buyers do you target?

The roles that hold speaking, training and coaching budgets in mid-market and enterprise companies:

  • Chief Human Resources Officers and Directors of People Operations
  • Chief Learning Officers and Learning & Development Managers
  • Employee Experience Leaders and Event Marketing Directors
  • Enterprise Business Unit Heads responsible for performance and programme purchasing

We then layer in company size, industry, technology stack signals, recent hiring and change triggers such as rapid headcount growth, leadership transitions or culture transformation, so each message reaches the right person at the right time. That lifts response rates and shortens the path to your first paid engagement.

Do I need to be famous or have a big social following?

No. You don't need to be a household name, a bestselling author or have hundreds of thousands of followers. Corporate buyers don't buy fame; they buy relevance, credibility and a proven ability to solve a specific business problem. A well-defined niche and a clear, results-focused message are worth far more to them than a large public audience.

Wherever you are in your career, we package your existing knowledge into proposals built for how companies buy. We have helped emerging speakers land their first five-figure corporate keynote, and established experts move from one-off bookings to multi-year enterprise retainers. Your expertise is the asset; we build the system that earns from it consistently.

How quickly does it go live, and when will opportunities arrive?
  • 21 to 30 days to set up: positioning workshops, ideal client profile, prospect database, multi-channel sequences and technical warmup so messages reach inboxes.
  • 30 to 60 days of live outreach: most clients start receiving qualified replies and booking discovery calls.
  • 60 to 90 days after launch: a full pipeline with engagements booked consistently. Corporate sales cycles usually run 30 to 90 days from first contact to signed contract.

The pipeline compounds: the longer it runs, the more touchpoints build up with key decision-makers and the better each outreach cycle converts.

Is there a long-term contract, or can I start with a pilot?

You start with a fixed pilot programme covering the full system setup, technical launch and first phase of active outreach. It lets you see the pipeline working, judge the quality of replies from corporate buyers and assess early momentum before committing further.

Once the pilot hits the response and qualified-call targets we agree together at the start, we move to flexible monthly agreements with clear, transparent renewal terms. No high-pressure, multi-year lock-ins. Our results should earn your partnership every month; we grow when you grow.

What do you need from me?

Time for the positioning workshops at kickoff, your existing material such as talks, frameworks and programmes, approval of your offers and messaging before launch, and your time on discovery calls once buyers reply. We handle the database, the outreach and the follow-up.

Who owns the contacts and relationships?

You do, entirely. Unlike an agency arrangement, the prospect data, the relationships and the pipeline we build belong to you, not to us.
